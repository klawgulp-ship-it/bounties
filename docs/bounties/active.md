## How to Claim a Bounty

1. Review the bounty description and requirements.
2. Comment on the relevant GitHub issue to express your intent to work on it.
3. Submit your work as a pull request referencing the issue.
4. Once reviewed and merged, the reward will be sent to your wallet.

> **Note:** Bounties are awarded at the discretion of the FL0X core team upon successful completion and review of the submitted work.
