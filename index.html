About Flipper.io

Flipper.io is an open-source project that connects contributors with active bounties hosted on Status Open Bounty & Ethlance. Our platform empowers developers, designers, and community members to collaborate, earn rewards, and drive meaningful progress in the decentralized ecosystem.
