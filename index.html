About Flipper.io

Flipper.io is an open-source, decentralized prediction market built on Ethereum. It enables users to create and participate in markets for real-world events, leveraging smart contracts for trustless and transparent settlement. The platform uses FL0X tokens for governance and rewards, and integrates with Status Open Bounty and Ethlance for community-driven development.
